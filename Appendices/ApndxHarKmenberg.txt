When the joint distribution is needed, three approaches are available:
1. Standard 2D transition matrix on the (m, p) grid, with cost O((K · Np )2 ).
2. Standard Monte Carlo, which naturally produces the joint sample {(mi , pi )}.
3. Harmenberg + reconstruction: compute aggregates via χ̃m , then reconstruct
the p-dimension from the model’s known age-conditional lognormal structure.

covt determines covt+1 through a deterministic multiplicative factor. Formula (24)
provides an independent way to verify this balanced-growth property numerically, using
only the 1D distribution at each period.
