1.2 Apparent Balanced Growth in c̄ and cov(c, p)

{sec:ApndxBalanced

Section 4.2 demonstrates some propositions under the assumption that, when an economy satisfies the GIC, there will be constant growth factors Ωc̄ and Ωcov respectively for
c̄ (the average value of the consumption ratio) and cov(c, p). In the case of a Szeidlinvariant economy, the main text shows that these are Ωc̄ = 1 and Ωcov = G. If the
economy is Harmenberg- but not Szeidl-invariant, no proof is offered that these growth
factors will be constant.
